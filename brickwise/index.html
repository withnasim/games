Brickwise: Block Puzzle

Block Puzzle · Free

Brickwise is a calm yet addictive block puzzle. Drag glossy blocks onto the 8×8 board, fill rows and columns to clear them, and chain combos for huge scores. There's no timer and no pressure, just you, the board and your best score.

Privacy policy

How to play Brickwise

  1. Drag blocks from the tray onto the 8×8 grid.
  2. Fill a full row or column to clear it.
  3. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move, to build big combos.
  4. When none of your blocks fit any more, the game ends. Think ahead and beat your high score!

Features

  • Play offline: on the bus, on a plane, anywhere without Wi-Fi
  • Combo streaks, multi-line bonuses and Perfect Clear rewards
  • Glossy, colorful blocks with smooth animations and satisfying sounds
  • Auto-save, so you can close the app and continue later
  • Small download that runs smoothly on older phones

Frequently asked questions

Is Brickwise free?

Yes. Brickwise is free to download and play on Google Play.

Can I play Brickwise offline?

Yes. Brickwise works fully offline, so you can play anywhere without an internet connection.

How do combos work in Brickwise?

Every line clear adds to your combo streak, and the streak multiplies your line bonus. The streak only breaks after three moves in a row without a clear.

Is Brickwise on iPhone?

Brickwise is on Android today. iPhone and iPad versions are planned.

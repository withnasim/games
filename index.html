Brickwise

Block Puzzle

A calm yet addictive block puzzle. Drag glossy blocks onto the board, clear rows and columns, and chain huge combos.

  • Drag blocks onto the 8×8 board and fill full lines to clear them
  • Combo streaks, multi-line bonuses and Perfect Clears
  • Saves automatically, so you can continue any time

Potion Pour

Color Sort Puzzle

Pour glowing potions from flask to flask until every flask holds a single color, then watch it get corked with a sparkle.

  • Endless levels, from easy to truly brain-bending
  • A new Daily Potion every day. Build your streak!
  • Magical scenes, color-blind friendly symbols, undo and hints

What is Instant Play?

Instant Play is where Najura puts games you can play straight from this website. Pick a game, tap Play and it starts in a few seconds. You don't need an app store, a sign-in or any free space on your phone.

Every game is made by Najura, the same small studio behind Brickwise, Potion Pour, Peep and Gilded on Google Play. We keep the games short to learn and quick to load, so they're good for a coffee break, a bus ride or a few minutes between tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What is Instant Play?

Instant Play is the part of Najura Games where you can play games directly in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install.

Are the games free?

Yes. Every Instant Play game is free to play.

Do the games work on phones?

Yes. The games run in modern mobile browsers on Android and iPhone, and on computers with Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Is my progress saved?

Your best score is saved in your browser on that device. Clearing your browser data resets it.

Whisker River

Hop a tabby cat across floating logs on a village river. Don't get wet.

Instant Play Free No download Phone & PC
Keyboard: ← → to change lane, ↑ or Space to jump. Phone: swipe and tap.

About Whisker River

Whisker River is a 3D endless runner set on a quiet village river at golden hour. Coconut palms and banana plants line the banks, thatched huts and haystacks sit in the green fields, and lotus flowers drift between the lily pads. Your orange tabby cat has one goal: get as far down the river as possible without touching the water.

Old tree trunks and banana-stem rafts float down three lanes. Some lanes run out, some have thorn bushes, and gaps open up between logs, so you need quick lane changes and well-timed jumps. The river speeds up the further you go.

How to play

← →Change lane. On a phone, swipe left or right. ↑ / SpaceJump. On a phone, tap the screen.
  1. Stay on a log or raft. If the cat lands on open water, it falls in and the run ends.
  2. Jump across the gaps between logs, and switch lanes when your log is about to end.
  3. Jump over thorn bushes. Running into one knocks the cat into the river.
  4. Collect gold coins along the logs and in the air above the gaps.

Tips for a longer run

  • Look two logs ahead, not just at the one under the cat. The safe lane is often decided early.
  • You can change lanes in mid-air. Jump first, then steer onto the next log.
  • Press jump slightly early. A jump pressed just before landing is remembered, so the cat bounces straight on.
  • Coins arranged in an arc over a gap show a jump that lands safely.
  • Banana rafts are just as safe as logs. Use them when the trunks run out.

Frequently asked questions

Is Whisker River free?

Yes. Whisker River is free to play in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs right on this page. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Can I play Whisker River on my phone?

Yes. It works in modern mobile browsers. Swipe left or right to change lanes and tap to jump.

Why does the cat fall in the water?

The cat falls when it lands where there is no log or raft, or when it runs into a thorn bush. Jump across gaps and switch lanes to keep it dry.

Is there a Whisker River app?

Not yet. Whisker River is an early browser version. A full version with more worlds is planned.

Frequently asked questions

When is Peep coming out?

Peep is almost ready and coming soon to Google Play.

Why does Peep need the microphone?

Peep listens so it can repeat what you say in its funny chick voice. The sound is processed on your device only: your voice is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and you can turn the microphone off at any time.

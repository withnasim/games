Gilded: Memory Card Match

Memory Card Game · Free

An elegant memory game for grown-ups. Turn gold-framed cards, find the matching jewel emblems and build long streaks on velvet Art Deco tables.

Privacy policy

How to play Gilded

  1. Tap a card to turn it over, then tap a second card.
  2. If the two emblems match, they stay face up. If not, they turn back, so remember where they were.
  3. Clear the table in as few moves as you can to earn up to three stars.

Features

  • 48 tables across four salons: classic matching, a race against the clock, look-alike twins in different jewels, and tables that shift when you slip
  • A Daily Challenge with the same table for every player, plus a daily streak
  • Zen mode with endless, untimed tables and soft lounge music
  • Streak multiplier up to ×5 and a memory score after every round
  • Works offline

Frequently asked questions

Is Gilded free?

Yes. Gilded is free to download and play on Google Play.

Can I play Gilded offline?

Yes. Gilded works fully offline, so you can play anywhere without an internet connection.

Who is Gilded for?

Gilded is designed for adults who enjoy calm, stylish brain games. Its content is suitable for everyone.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day there is a new table that is the same for every player. Finish it to grow your daily streak.

Is Gilded on iPhone?

Gilded is on Android today. iPhone and iPad versions are planned.

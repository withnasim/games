Potion Pour: Color Sort Puzzle

Color Sort Puzzle · Free

Potion Pour is a calm, beautiful color sorting puzzle. Pour glowing potions from flask to flask until every flask holds a single color, then watch it get corked with a sparkle.

Privacy policy

How to play Potion Pour

  1. Tap a flask to pick it up, then tap another flask to pour.
  2. Potion only pours onto the same color, or into an empty flask.
  3. Fill every flask with a single color to complete the level.

Features

  • Endless levels that grow from easy to truly brain-bending
  • Every level is checked by a solver, so it can always be solved
  • A new Daily Potion every day. Build your streak!
  • Earn up to 3 stars per level and unlock magical scenes
  • Undo, hints, a spare flask and color-blind friendly symbols
  • Works fully offline, with no account needed

Frequently asked questions

Is Potion Pour free?

Yes. Potion Pour is free to download and play on Google Play.

Can I play Potion Pour offline?

Yes. Potion Pour works fully offline.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before you play it, so there is always a solution.

Is Potion Pour color-blind friendly?

Yes. You can turn on symbols that mark each potion color.
